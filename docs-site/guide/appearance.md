# Appearance & Customization

DocuShark is built to bend around how *you* like to work. Almost every visual
choice — colours, spacing, motion, even the shape of your text cursor — lives in
one place: **Settings → Appearance**. This page is a tour of what you can change.

Open it from the **Settings** button in the toolbar, then the **Appearance** tab.

![Settings → Appearance: the theme builder with Base (System / Light / Dark), colour presets including "Surprise me", and the Primary, CTA, Surface, and Text colour fields — with the prose backdrop and caret options below.](/screenshots/settings-appearance.png)

::: tip Appearance vs. Styling
**Appearance** changes the *app around your work* — themes, spacing, the editor.
To style the *shapes in a diagram* (fills, strokes, fonts, reusable style
profiles), see [Styling & Themes](/guide/styling).
:::

## Theme

The theme builder is the centrepiece. You don't just pick light or dark — you can
build your own.

- **Base** — **System**, **Light**, or **Dark**. System follows your device.
  You edit Light and Dark **independently**, so your light theme and dark theme
  can each look exactly how you want; switch base to customise the other.
- **Presets** — start from a ready-made colour set, then tweak. Feeling
  adventurous? Hit **Surprise me** to generate a random, still-legible theme.
- **Fine-tune the colours** — set your **Primary**, **CTA**, **Surface**, and
  **Text** colours. DocuShark checks contrast as you go and shows a gentle
  **Low contrast** warning if a choice would be hard to read, so your custom
  theme always stays legible.
- **Reset** the current base back to its default any time.

Your **diagrams keep their own colours** — the theme changes the app's surfaces
and chrome, not the shapes you've drawn, so a diagram looks the same in light or
dark.

## Prose background

Choose the **backdrop behind the writing area** from a set of presets that follow
your theme colours — from a clean flat surface to something with a little more
warmth.

## Caret

Tune the text cursor in the writing editor:

- **Style** — a thin **Bar** or a **Block** over the character.
- **Smooth writing** — glide the caret between positions as you type instead of
  jumping (automatically off when motion is reduced).
- **Colour** — defaults to your theme's text colour; override it if you like.

## Spelling

Pick how spellcheck works while you write:

- **Custom** — DocuShark's own dictionary, with **Add to dictionary**.
- **System** — your browser or OS spellchecker.
- **Off** — no spellchecking.

## Tables

Toggle **rounded tables** on for softer corners in the writing editor, or off for
a square, grid-style look.

## Motion

Set **interface animations** to **Full**, **Reduced**, or **System** (which
follows your device's accessibility setting). Reduced motion also quiets related
effects like the smooth caret.

## Density

Tighten or loosen spacing across the whole app:

- **Compact** — fit more on screen.
- **Normal** — the default.
- **Spacious** — roomier spacing and larger targets.

## Interface size

A slider that **scales the entire interface** up or down — handy on very large or
very small displays. The canvas and your diagrams are *not* affected, so zooming
the UI never changes your drawing.

## Canvas grid

Set the **grid opacity** — how visible the canvas grid is, from fully visible down
to hidden (0%).

## Layout

The **Layout** section is embedded right here in Appearance — it's the panel
arrangement editor for each [layout mode](/guide/layout-modes). Dock the document
and properties panels where you want them, per layout.

## Window title bar (desktop)

On the desktop app, you can replace your operating system's window title bar with
**DocuShark's own** for a more unified look. It's off by default, and the app
restarts to apply. *(This option only appears on the desktop app.)*

## Mobile preview

An **experimental, early-access layout** for small touch screens. Turn it on to
try DocuShark's mobile layer; it only takes effect on a touch device with a small
screen. See [Mobile Preview](/guide/mobile-preview).

## Reset

At the bottom, **Reset appearance to defaults** returns theme, motion, density,
interface size, and layout customisation to their original settings in one click.
