# Tutorial: A Content Creator's Plan

Running a channel means keeping ideas, a calendar, a production process, and your
sources straight — usually across five different apps. This walkthrough puts them
in one DocuShark document: a **science-explainer channel content plan** with a
topic mind-map and a production pipeline.

**What you'll build:** a channel plan — content pillars, a publishing calendar, a
performance table, a topic **mind-map**, and a **production-pipeline** diagram.

**You'll learn:** the **Add Group mind-map**, **linking a diagram** page, tables
for planning, light citations for fact-checks, and working across devices.

**Before you start:** [open DocuShark](https://app.docushark.app).

## 1. Set up the plan

Click **New**, name it `Curious Minds — Channel Content Plan`, and add **document
fields** for `Channel`, `Quarter`, and `Host`. Write a short positioning line and
your **content pillars** as a list — the recurring themes every video ladders up
to. See [Document Fields](/guide/document-fields).

## 2. Build the publishing calendar

Plan releases in a **table** — a row per video, columns for Date, Topic, Format,
and Status. Update Status (`Scripting` → `Editing` → `Published`) as you go, so
the whole quarter is visible at a glance.

| Date | Topic | Format | Status |
|------|-------|--------|--------|
| Oct 4 | Why is the sky dark at night? | Explainer | Published |
| Nov 1 | How we weighed the Earth | How We Know | Scripting |

## 3. Map your topics with a mind-map

Ideas cluster better visually. On a prose page, right-click and choose
**Add Group…** to drop in a **mind-map**, then branch each **pillar** into
episode ideas. It's quick to reshuffle as the backlog grows — far nicer than a
flat list for spotting gaps and themes.

## 4. Diagram your production pipeline

Add a **second canvas page** named `Production Pipeline` and lay out your
workflow with connectors (`C`): *Ideate → Research → Script → Record → Edit →
Thumbnail → Publish → Promote → Analyze* — and loop **Analyze back to Ideate** so
the diagram captures the feedback cycle. Link it from your prose so collaborators
know the process. See [Multi-Page Documents](/guide/multi-page-documents).

## 5. Track what's working

Add a **performance** table — past episodes with views, average watch time, and a
note (*"lost people at the math — trim next time"*). Keeping the numbers next to
the plan turns each quarter's review into a two-minute read.

## 6. Keep your facts honest

A science channel lives on accuracy. For claims you cite on-screen, keep a small
**reference library** and drop **inline citations** into your notes — a primary
source per episode, always to hand. See [Citations & References](/guide/citations).

## 7. Plan on the couch, publish from the desk

Your plan syncs across devices — jot an idea on your **phone**, flesh it out at
your desk. Install DocuShark as a PWA and try the
[mobile layout](/guide/mobile-preview); mark the plan
[available offline](/guide/offline-and-sync) so ideas never wait for signal.

## What you learned

You built a channel plan that holds your pillars, calendar, topic map, production
pipeline, performance log, and sources — one synced document instead of five apps.
